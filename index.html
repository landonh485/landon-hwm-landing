Why this matters here

In southwest Virginia, water damage doesn't start in the basement. It starts at the roofline.

Our clay-heavy soil swells when wet and shrinks when dry — which means every drop your gutters dump at the foundation works the ground under your house, season after season.

A clogged or undersized gutter system doesn't announce itself. It quietly pushes water exactly where you don't want it:

clogged gutter → overflow at the roofline → saturated clay soil → hydrostatic pressure → foundation wall → basement / crawlspace → mold
